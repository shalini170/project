## Title of the Project
The AI-Powered Legal Agreement Analyzer is a web-based application that uses Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze legal agreements. It identifies important clauses, highlights potential risks, detects unusual terms, and provides simplified explanations of complex legal language.
## About
<!--Detailed Description about the project-->
The AI-Powered Legal Agreement Analyzer is an intelligent web-based application designed to simplify the process of understanding and analyzing legal agreements using Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP). Legal documents often contain complex terminology and lengthy clauses that can be difficult for ordinary users to understand. This project aims to make legal document analysis easier, faster, and more accessible.
## Features
<!--List the features of the project as shown below-->

1. Document Upload – Upload legal agreements in PDF or DOCX format.
2. Clause Extraction – Identify important clauses in legal documents.
3. Risk Detection – Highlight potentially risky or unfavorable clauses.
4. AI-Based Summarization – Generate a simple summary of the agreement.
5. Legal Term Explanation – Explain complicated legal terminology in simple English.
6. Agreement Analysis – Provide insights into important terms and conditions.

Component	Technology

3. Functional Requirements
1. User registration and login.
2. Upload legal agreements in PDF or DOCX format.
3. Extract text from uploaded documents.
4. Identify and classify important legal clauses.
5. Detect potentially risky or unfavorable terms.
6. Generate simplified summaries of agreements.
7. Explain complex legal terms in simple language.
8. Display the analysis results to users.
4. Non-Functional Requirements
1. Performance: Analyze documents within a reasonable time.
2. Security: Protect uploaded legal documents and user information.
3. Usability: Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.
4. Accuracy: Generate relevant summaries and clause analysis.
5. Reliability: Handle document uploads and analysis consistently.
6. Scalability: Support additional users and document types in the future.

## Results and Impact
<!--Give the results and impact as shown below-->
Results and Impact
The AI-Powered Legal Agreement Analyzer helps users understand legal documents more easily by automating document analysis using Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing.
Results
1. Successfully extracts text from uploaded PDF and DOCX legal agreements.
2. Identifies important clauses such as payment, confidentiality, and termination.
3. Detects potentially risky or unfavorable terms.
4. Generates simplified summaries of complex legal documents.
5. Explains difficult legal terminology in simple English.
6. Provides a detailed risk assessment and clause analysis report.
Impact
- Time Saving: Reduces the time required for manual legal document review.
- Easy Understanding: Makes complex legal agreements easier to understand.
- Risk Awareness: Helps users identify potentially unfavorable conditions.
- Improved Accessibility: Makes basic legal document analysis accessible to non-legal users.
- Better Decision-Making: Helps users make more informed decisions before signing agreements.
